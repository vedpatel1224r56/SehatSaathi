Sawaal jo aapke mann mein hain

Kya yeh doctor ka replacement hai?

Bilkul nahi. SehatSaathi aapko diagnose nahi karta, treat nahi karta, aur prescribe nahi karta. Yeh sirf aapki report ko samajhne mein madad karta hai — kya dhyan dena hai, kya stable hai, aur doctor se kya poochhna hai. Doctor appointment cancel mat karo.

Meri report aur data safe hai?

Haan. Aapka data sirf aapke account se connected hai. Koi bhi without your permission access nahi kar sakta. Aap kabhi bhi apna data export ya delete kar sakte hain.

Kaunse reports upload ho sakte hain?

Blood panel, HbA1c, CBC, thyroid (TSH, T3, T4), lipid profile, kidney function (creatinine, urea), liver function, vitamin D, B12 — practically sab kuch. PDF ya phone camera se photo — dono kaam karta hai. iPhone HEIC photos bhi supported hain.

Kya Hindi mein kaam karta hai?

Abhi app English mein hai lekin doctor ke liye sawaal aur summaries simple English mein hain jo samajhne mein easy hain. Gujarati aur Hindi support coming soon. Vadodara pilot ke liye Gujarati onboarding available hai.

Lab ke liye kya kaam hai?

Lab ke billing counter ya report collection desk pe ek QR code print karo. Patient scan kare aur seedha SehatSaathi pe aajaye. 14-din ke baad pilot data aapko milega — kitne patients ne upload kiya, kitno ne summary dekhi, feedback kya tha. Koi HMS change, koi staff training, koi cost nahi.

Kya yeh government health records se connect hota hai?

ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) link karne ka option hai — optional hai. Agar link karte hain toh report aur profile better connected hote hain. Required nahi hai.
