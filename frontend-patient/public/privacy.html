Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 July 2026  ·  Last updated: 10 June 2026  ·  Version 1.0

⚕️ SehatSaathi is not a medical device SehatSaathi explains your lab report in plain language. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or treat any medical condition. All outputs are for informational purposes only. Always consult a qualified doctor before making any health decisions.

1. Who we are

SehatSaathi ("we", "our", "us") is a health report companion application. The data controller for your personal data is the operator of SehatSaathi. For any data-related request, contact us at pved2038@gmail.com.

3. How we use your data

  • Generate plain-language explanations of your lab results
  • Build a personalised weekly action plan
  • Prepare questions for your next doctor visit
  • Track changes in your health markers across multiple reports
  • Send you check-in reminders (only if you opt in)
  • Troubleshoot technical errors in the app

We do not: sell your data, share it with advertisers, use it to train AI models owned by third parties, or use it for any purpose other than providing the SehatSaathi service to you.

7. Your rights under DPDP Act 2023

As a data principal you have the following rights, which you can exercise from within the app (Settings → Privacy) or by emailing pved2038@gmail.com:

  • Right to access — download all your personal data
  • Right to correction — update your profile details at any time
  • Right to erasure — delete your account and all associated data
  • Right to withdraw consent — you may withdraw consent at any time; this will not affect prior processing
  • Right to grievance redressal — we will respond to data requests within 30 days

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you within 72 hours by email or in-app notification.

8. Children's privacy

SehatSaathi is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 without verifiable parental consent. If you believe a child under 18 has created an account, contact us immediately at pved2038@gmail.com and we will delete the account.

9. Security

We implement the following security measures to protect your data:

  • All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS 1.2+
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt (cost factor 12)
  • Authentication uses signed JWT tokens with short expiry
  • Database is encrypted at rest on the hosting provider's infrastructure
  • Uploaded files are stored on an isolated, private volume — not publicly accessible
  • Rate limiting is applied to all public API endpoints to prevent abuse

10. Cookies and analytics

SehatSaathi uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate usage patterns (pages visited, session duration). Google Analytics does not receive your name, phone number, or health data. Analytics data is anonymised. You can opt out by disabling JavaScript or using a browser content blocker.

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels.

11. Changes to this policy

We will notify you of material changes via the app or email at least 7 days before the change takes effect. Continued use after that date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. You may always delete your account if you disagree with any change.
