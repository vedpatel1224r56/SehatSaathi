# SehatSaathi Patient Onboarding (English)

## What this app does
- Helps you understand uploaded lab reports in plain language.
- Shows what may be worth discussing, what looks stable, and what questions to carry to your doctor.
- Lets you save one reading, symptom, medicine note, or question between reports.
- Keeps your report history together so your next follow-up is easier to prepare.

## How to use during the pilot
1. Create your account and accept the consent screen.
2. Complete the basic profile details that help report summaries stay personal.
3. Upload a clear PDF or photo of your lab report, or open the link shared by the lab.
4. Start on Today to see the main follow-up point and doctor questions.
5. Open Reports to review the uploaded report, trends, and saved doctor questions.
6. Use Actions to save one quick update such as BP, glucose, weight, symptom, medicine, or question.
7. If anything looks confusing, use Help and support in Profile or message the pilot support number on WhatsApp.

## What SehatSaathi does not do
- It does not diagnose any condition.
- It does not replace your doctor.
- It does not prescribe medicines or treatment.
- It does not decide whether something is an emergency.

## Safety note
For chest pain, breathing difficulty, fainting, seizures, uncontrolled bleeding, severe weakness, or any symptom that worries you, contact a doctor or emergency service immediately. Do not wait for app guidance.

## Data and privacy
Your profile, consent, uploaded reports, extracted values, saved actions, and support messages are stored for continuity and safety. You can request export or deletion of your data from the app.
