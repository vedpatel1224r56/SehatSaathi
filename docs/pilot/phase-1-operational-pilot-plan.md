# SehatSaathi Phase 1 Operational Pilot Plan

## Pilot Goal

Run a small, founder-led pilot that proves one thing:

> Patients understand their report more clearly and come better prepared for follow-up.

This is a closed pilot, not a public launch.

---

## Pilot City

### Primary location

- Vadodara

### Why Vadodara

- founder can support users directly
- easier partner coordination
- faster issue resolution
- easier repeat contact for follow-up

---

## First 50 Users

### Target composition

- 20 users from 2 local labs
- 10 users from 1 hospital / OPD follow-up desk
- 10 users from 1 pharmacy / repeat chronic patients
- 10 founder-recruited direct users from known circles

### Who these 50 users should be

Prioritize:

- diabetes / sugar report users
- CBC / fatigue / low-energy report users
- thyroid / cholesterol repeat-report users
- family-managed healthcare users
- people who already have a recent report in hand

Avoid in the first 50:

- emergency or unstable cases
- users without any report
- users expecting diagnosis or prescription
- users who are not comfortable using WhatsApp / smartphone basics

---

## Exact First-50 Breakdown

### Batch 1: Founder-observed users

- 10 users
- source: direct founder onboarding
- goal: watch real upload behavior and fix friction fast

### Batch 2: Lab QR users

- 20 users
- source: 2 partner labs
- goal: test real post-report clarity behavior

### Batch 3: Follow-up desk / clinic users

- 10 users
- source: hospital or clinic follow-up area
- goal: test doctor handoff usefulness

### Batch 4: Chronic repeat users

- 10 users
- source: pharmacy / direct referrals
- goal: test continuity, plan return, and retest guidance

---

## From Where They Come

### Channel 1: Lab report collection desk

Use for:

- fresh report users
- HbA1c / CBC / lipid / thyroid / kidney / liver report uploads

Staff line:

> If you want help understanding your report clearly, you can scan this.

### Channel 2: Hospital / clinic follow-up desk

Use for:

- users already planning doctor review
- patients who need a cleaner summary before the visit

Staff line:

> This can help you review what to discuss before the next appointment.

### Channel 3: Pharmacy referrals

Use for:

- repeat chronic users
- users already carrying sugar / thyroid / BP history

Staff line:

> If you already have reports and want to stay organized between visits, this may help.

### Channel 4: Founder direct onboarding

Use for:

- first 10 users
- quick feedback loop
- bug capture and UX refinement

---

## Who Explains The Product

### Primary explainer for the first 50

- Founder only

This is the safest approach for phase 1.

### Secondary explainers

- one trusted front-desk staff member at each partner site
- one lab staff member who only uses the one-line intro

### What staff should say

Only this:

> Upload your report here if you want help understanding what may be worth following up on.

They should not explain:

- diagnosis
- treatment
- medicine advice
- urgency beyond what the app says

---

## Who Supports Users If Something Fails

### Level 1 support

- Founder on WhatsApp and phone

Use for:

- upload problems
- login confusion
- report not parsing clearly
- “what do I do next?” confusion

Response target:

- same day during pilot

### Level 2 support

- one ops/admin backup person

Use for:

- partner coordination
- repeat complaints
- account/profile issues
- ABHA verification follow-up

### Clinical fallback

- doctor / lab / clinic remains the medical fallback

Use this line:

> If the report feels unclear, serious, or worrying, please use the original report and consult your doctor.

---

## Pilot Support Workflow

1. User scans QR
2. User uploads report
3. If upload works:
   - founder asks one question:
   - `Was this easier to understand than reading the report alone?`
4. If upload fails:
   - founder checks image/PDF quality
   - asks user to retry with clearer file if needed
   - logs the failure in pilot sheet
5. If summary feels wrong or too weak:
   - founder marks it as product-review feedback
   - user is told to rely on the original report and clinician review

---

## Daily Operating Rhythm

### Founder daily responsibilities

- onboard 3 to 5 users personally
- observe at least 2 real uploads
- capture every failure point
- reply to every support message
- review day-end numbers

### End-of-day checklist

- new users onboarded
- reports uploaded
- summaries successfully viewed
- plans opened
- doctor handoffs opened
- retest guidance opened
- failures logged
- support issues closed

---

## What Counts As A Failure

Log separately:

- upload failed
- extraction weak
- summary felt generic
- plan felt irrelevant
- retest timing felt confusing
- doctor handoff not useful
- user did not understand next step

---

## Success Criteria For The First 50

### Must-have

- 35 of 50 users upload successfully
- 30 of 50 say the summary made the report easier to understand
- 15 of 50 open Action Plan or Doctor Handoff

### Strong signal

- 10 of 50 return within 7 days
- 5 of 50 use retest guidance meaningfully
- at least 1 partner asks to continue pilot

---

## Pilot Roles

### Founder

- product explainer
- first-line support
- feedback collector
- daily metrics owner

### Partner staff

- point user to QR
- do not clinically interpret app output
- escalate stuck users to founder

### Doctor / clinic

- optional follow-up reviewer
- medical fallback when user needs real interpretation

## WhatsApp Support Template

Hi, this is SehatSaathi support. Please send:

1. a screenshot of the issue
2. whether the report was image or PDF
3. what step you got stuck on

If the report feels medically urgent or worrying, please rely on the original report and contact your doctor directly.

---

## Brutal Rule

If the founder is not personally close to the first 50 users, the pilot will produce weak learning.

For phase 1, closeness matters more than scale.
