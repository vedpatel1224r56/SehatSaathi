## 4. Demo Script

### 60-Second Demo

1. Open SehatSaathi home screen.
2. Say:
   “This is not a diagnosis app. It is a follow-up companion after a report is uploaded.”
3. Upload one lab report.
4. Show the report screen and say:
   “First, it highlights what may deserve attention, what can wait, and what questions may help during doctor follow-up.”
5. Open the Action Plan and say:
   “Then it prepares one practical follow-up plan based on the report, without turning this into a generic wellness app.”
6. Open Review Prep / Doctor Handoff and say:
   “This makes the next consultation easier because the patient comes in more prepared.”
7. Open Retest Guidance and say:
   “It also explains whether retesting may be useful, when, and why, instead of pushing random bookings.”
8. Close with:
   “The goal is simple: keep health follow-up clear between visits.”

### 30-Second Demo Version

“A patient uploads a report. SehatSaathi explains what deserves attention first, prepares one calm follow-up plan, and keeps the next doctor visit easier to manage. It is built for continuity after diagnostics, not panic and not generic wellness.”

## 5. Founder Pitch

### 30-Second Founder Pitch

I’m building SehatSaathi, a health follow-up platform for the moment after a patient receives a report. Most people get lab results but still do not know what matters, what to do next, or when to follow up. SehatSaathi turns reports into clarity, follow-up guidance, retest intelligence, and doctor-ready continuity. We are not trying to replace doctors or become another broad healthcare marketplace. We are building the continuity layer between reports and visits.

### 60-Second Founder Pitch

I’m building SehatSaathi because the problem doesn’t start when someone books a test. It starts after the report arrives. Patients often leave with a PDF, a few abnormal values, and a lot of confusion. They don’t know what matters first, what can wait, what to ask during follow-up, or whether a retest is actually useful.

SehatSaathi is designed for that exact moment. A patient uploads a report, gets a calm explanation, sees what deserves attention first, receives a practical follow-up plan, and stays organized between visits. Over time, the platform helps with retest timing, continuity, and doctor handoff.

So our wedge is not diagnostics commerce. It is report understanding plus follow-up continuity.

## 14-Day Pilot Ask

### What to Ask From a Lab or Hospital

- One QR poster near billing or report collection
- Permission to run a 14-day pilot
- Staff line:
  “If you want help understanding your report clearly, you can scan this.”

### What You Are Not Asking For

- no workflow change
- no system integration
- no clinical replacement
- no staff training burden

### What You Will Share Back

- number of scans
- uploads completed
- summary views
- follow-up plan starts
- patient feedback

---

## First 100 Users

### Goal

Prove that patients feel clearer within the first 30 seconds after upload.

### Target Mix

- 40 report-upload users from lab QR flow
- 20 users from doctor referrals
- 20 users from pharmacy referrals
- 20 users from founder-led direct onboarding

### Metrics

- QR scans
- accounts created
- reports uploaded
- summaries viewed
- action plans opened
- doctor handoff opened
- retest guidance opened
- day-2 return
- day-7 return
- support requests

### Pass Condition

- 60% say the report became easier to understand
- 25% open plan or doctor handoff
- 10% return within 7 days
