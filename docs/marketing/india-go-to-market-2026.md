# SehatSaathi India Go-To-Market Prototype

Date: 6 June 2026

## Market position

SehatSaathi is not an AI doctor and should never be marketed as one.

Category:

> The continuity experience attached to every lab report.

Patient promise:

> Your report arrived. Clarity should too.

Lab promise:

> Your PDF is delivered. Your patient relationship should not end.

The emotional product being sold is not analysis. It is relief, preparedness, and remembered context.

## Why this route fits India

- Meta reported in September 2025 that 91% of online adults in India chat with businesses weekly.
- Meta states that WhatsApp business messaging should be expected, consented, useful, and frequency-controlled.
- Government data published in March 2026 reported more than 86 crore ABHAs, more than 90 crore linked health records, and over 2.5 lakh facilities using ABDM-enabled software.
- The market is digitally ready, but the first SehatSaathi wedge should remain simple: lab trust, QR discovery, WhatsApp support, report clarity, and measurable follow-up preparation.

## Launch strategy

### Phase 1: Founder proof, days 1-14

Target:

- 2 diagnostic labs
- 50 patients
- 10 observed uploads
- 5 doctor reactions

Offer:

> We will run a 14-day branded patient clarity pilot. No LIS integration, no staff training, and no clinical responsibility shift.

Lab receives:

- branded QR campaign URL
- one front-desk sentence
- founder-led patient support
- end-of-pilot report

Measure:

- report-page visits
- account starts
- reports uploaded
- successful parses
- doctor questions viewed
- WhatsApp shares
- day-7 return
- support cases
- patient clarity score

Pass:

- 60% of successful uploads say the report feels clearer
- 30% view or share doctor questions
- 20% return or save an action within 7 days
- at least one lab asks to continue

### Phase 2: Repeatable city playbook, days 15-45

Target:

- 10 labs or clinics in Vadodara
- 300 patient journeys
- 3 repeatable acquisition sources

Channels:

1. Founder visits with a live report demo.
2. QR at billing and report collection.
3. Lab WhatsApp report-delivery message.
4. Doctor and dietitian referral links.
5. Family forwarding on WhatsApp.

Do not run broad paid ads yet. Paid traffic before conversion proof will buy curiosity, not trust.

### Phase 3: Public proof, days 46-90

Only after pilot proof:

- Publish one partner case study.
- Publish one patient story with explicit consent.
- Create Gujarati and Hindi campaign variants.
- Run tightly targeted click-to-WhatsApp ads within partner lab catchment areas.
- Recruit 25 founding labs in Gujarat.
- Start LinkedIn founder-led sales content aimed at lab owners, pathologists, and clinic operators.

## Sales scripts

### Front-desk sentence

> If you want help understanding what to discuss from your report, scan this.

### Lab-owner opener

> You already deliver the report. SehatSaathi gives the patient a branded clarity experience after delivery without changing your current workflow.

### WhatsApp outreach

> Hi, I am Ved, founder of SehatSaathi. We help diagnostic labs turn report delivery into a clearer patient follow-up experience. I am inviting a small number of Vadodara labs to a 14-day pilot with no LIS integration or staff retraining. Can I show you the patient experience in 10 minutes?

### Objection: “Are you replacing doctors?”

> No. SehatSaathi does not diagnose or prescribe. It explains report context, keeps questions ready, and helps the patient arrive better prepared for clinician review.

### Objection: “We already send PDFs on WhatsApp.”

> Keep doing that. We attach a useful continuity experience to the same report moment. The PDF remains the source report.

### Objection: “My staff cannot handle another system.”

> The pilot requires one QR or one link. We personally support the first patients and measure the result.

## Pricing tests

Do not lead with per-AI-call pricing.

### Founding pilot

- 14 days
- up to 100 patient journeys
- setup and founder support included
- free or ₹2,500 refundable commitment fee

The commitment fee filters casual interest. Refund it if agreed pilot support or uptime is not delivered.

### Starter lab

- ₹4,999/month
- up to 500 delivered continuity journeys
- lab branding
- engagement summary
- support

### Growth lab

- ₹9,999/month
- up to 1,500 journeys
- custom campaign links
- branch comparison
- monthly review

Pricing should be validated with real lab interviews before becoming contractual.

## Content engine

Weekly founder content:

- Monday: one patient confusion pattern
- Wednesday: one 20-second product transformation
- Friday: one pilot learning or lab metric

Content rules:

- never post medical interpretation as clickbait
- never imply diagnosis
- never use fake testimonials
- never publish patient data without explicit consent
- show the product outcome, not “AI technology”

## Dashboard metrics

North-star pilot metric:

> Percentage of uploaded reports that lead to one useful continuity action.

Supporting metrics:

- campaign visit to account-start rate
- account-start to report-upload rate
- upload success rate
- summary viewed rate
- doctor-question share rate
- action saved rate
- day-7 return
- support rate per 100 journeys
- lab lead to meeting rate
- meeting to pilot rate
- pilot to paid continuation rate

## Brutal scale rules

Scale if:

- at least 2 of the first 5 labs continue or refer another lab
- more than 50% of successful upload users report increased clarity
- more than 20% complete a continuity action
- support burden is below 15 cases per 100 journeys

Pause and fix if:

- users repeatedly ask whether SehatSaathi is diagnosing them
- report parsing success falls below 80% on partner formats
- labs cannot explain the product in one sentence
- doctor questions feel generic
- the lab sees no retention or brand value after 300 journeys

Do not chase national attention before one local partner can say:

> Patients found this useful, it did not burden our staff, and we want to continue.
